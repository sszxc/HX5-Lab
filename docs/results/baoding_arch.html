Baoding v4: teacher and student networks

A PPO teacher learns the task from noisy ball positions. DAgger then distills it into a student that sees only the wrist camera and what the real hand measures. Deployed student: --enc ssm --stack 3. Code: robotics/policies/rl/baoding_noisy_train.py (teacher), baoding_dagger.py (student), baoding_noisy_env.py (observations). 20 joints, 20 Hz. Image shapes are H×W×C.
